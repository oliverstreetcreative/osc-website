What we’d tell you not to buy

Not a brand video. Skip the drone shots and the voiceover about your values. People don’t trust a company talking about itself - that’s what the 88% means.

Not a scripted customer. We ask questions and let your customer answer in their own words. If you write it for them, people can tell.

Not a bought one. Since October 2024 the FTC bans fake testimonials and paying people for good reviews. That’s good news if your customers already like you!

FTC, Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials, 16 CFR Part 465, effective October 21, 2024.
